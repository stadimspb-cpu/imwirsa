The Short Version

In short

These terms cover imwirsa.org and MWApp, both operated by International Maritime Welfare Innovation Research and Support Association (IMWIRSA), a non-profit association established in Estonia. By using either, you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use the site or the app.

🆓

Free, no account

MWApp is free for seafarers, with no registration and no subscription for the core features.

🚧

Still being built

MWApp is an active prototype. Port information is added and corrected as coordinators submit it — treat it as a helpful guide, not a guarantee.

🧭

Not official advice

Nothing here replaces instructions from your Master, your agent, your union, or local port and immigration authorities.

Last updated: 12 August 2026. This is an early version of these terms, written alongside MWApp itself — it will be refined as the platform develops.

Ground Rules

Using MWApp & this site

Who this is for

MWApp is built for seafarers and the coordinators, unions and welfare organisations who support them. The website is open to anyone — seafarers, partners, researchers, journalists, or anyone curious about our work.

No account required

MWApp works without registration. Where a Trade Union or workplace-organisation code unlocks Premium services, entering that code is the only "account-like" step in the app, and it's optional.

Availability

We aim to keep MWApp and this website available, but we don't guarantee uninterrupted access. Both can go offline for maintenance, updates, or reasons outside our control — a port's Wi-Fi being one of them.

Please Read This Part

Content & accuracy

Port information is gathered and verified by local coordinators, but places, prices, hours and rules all change — sometimes faster than we can update a Port Card.

Provided as-is

MWApp and this website are provided "as is" and "as available," without warranties of any kind, express or implied — including that the information is complete, accurate, or current at the moment you read it.

Not official or legal advice

Nothing in MWApp or on this website is immigration, legal, medical or safety advice. Shore Pass rules, visa requirements, port security procedures and similar matters are set by local authorities and can change without notice — always confirm with your ship's agent, your Master, or the relevant authority before relying on anything here.

Third-party places & services

Shops, pharmacies, taxi companies, seafarers' centres and other places listed in MWApp are independent of IMWIRSA. We do our best to keep listings accurate through our coordinators, but we don't control these businesses and aren't responsible for their prices, service, or conduct.

Emergencies

In a genuine emergency, always use the local emergency number (for example, 112 in much of Europe) or contact your ship directly — don't wait on an app, including this one.

Playing Fair

Acceptable use & intellectual property

What we ask of you

Please don't use MWApp or this website to break the law, harass or impersonate someone, interfere with how the app or site works, or try to extract, scrape or resell our port data in bulk without asking us first.

You're welcome to share and cite our published research with attribution.

What you share with us

If you send us something — a message through the contact form, a completed port questionnaire, feedback on the app — you're giving us permission to use it for the purpose you sent it for (answering you, building the relevant Port Card, improving MWApp). See our Privacy Policy for how we handle any personal data involved.
