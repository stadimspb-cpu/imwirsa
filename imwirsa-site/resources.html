IMWIRSA Practice and Resources for Seafarer Welfare

EN RU

Practical guidance, tools, and materials for professionals and volunteers working on seafarer welfare.

Practice Guides
Field-Tested, Adapted for Europe

Practice Guides

Methodological materials originally developed through years of hands-on seafarer welfare work, reviewed and adapted by IMWIRSA to align with EU law and current international maritime standards. Where Research sets out the evidence, these guides turn it into something a volunteer or coordinator can use on a ship visit.

📘

IMWIRSA Ship Visitor Handbook

Code of Conduct and practical guide for volunteers and coordinators: preparing for a visit, on-board communication, recognising distress, ship-type specifics, GDPR-compliant reporting, and the safeguarding escalation route.

Revision 2.1 · August 2026 · Original methodological development: 2022 · Updated and adapted for IMWIRSA: 2026
Download (EN) → Download (RU) →
🎓

IMWIRSA Ship Visitor Training Course

A full training programme for preparing ship visitors: seafarers' rights under MLC-2006, cross-cultural and interreligious communication, on-board safety protocols, a dedicated safeguarding and professional-boundaries section, GDPR-aligned data protection for visitor reporting, a step-by-step crisis response protocol, and a practical part with 19 training exercises and 16 group discussion scenarios.

Edition 2.2 · August 2026 · Original methodological development: 2020 · Updated and adapted for IMWIRSA: 2026
