News & Analysis Issue 001 July 2026

Crew abandonment reached a record in 2025 — and 2026 is running ahead of it

More seafarers were left without wages, supplies or a way home last year than in any year since the joint database opened in 2004. In 185 of those cases, the insurance that international law has required since 2017 was not there.
