Who We Are

About IMWIRSA

IMWIRSA — the International Maritime Welfare Innovation Research and Support Association — is an independent, non-profit international initiative headquartered in Tallinn, Estonia, bringing together research, innovation and practical solutions to advance a modern system of seafarer welfare.

We study seafarers' real needs, track changes in the port and maritime environment, and develop solutions that make shore-based support more accessible, relevant and effective.

We don't replace the seafarers' centres, missions and chaplaincies that have supported sailors for generations — we connect them. Every partner keeps its name, its funding and its independence; IMWIRSA provides the shared digital layer, research and coordination that lets the whole network work as one.

IMWIRSA's work is built on the interaction of research, local expertise, digital tools and international cooperation.

🔬

Research

Studying seafarers' needs, monitoring changes in seafarers' welfare, analysing existing problems and practices, running surveys, and developing the Research Observatory.

💡

Innovation

Developing and testing new approaches and digital tools, including MWApp, Port Cards and other solutions that improve seafarers' access to information and welfare services.

🤝

Support

Helping to develop practical shore-based support through cooperation with seafarers' centres, missions, unions, charities, port welfare services and other partners.

From Practical Experience to a Global Initiative

Our Story

IMWIRSA grew out of years of hands-on experience in seafarer welfare, and the recognition that many shore-based welfare challenges cannot be solved by individual centres, missions or port organisations acting alone.

The COVID-19 pandemic exposed the fragility of the existing system with particular clarity. Shore-leave restrictions, changing port regulations, the closure or reduced operation of seafarers' centres, and rapidly outdated information all made it harder and harder for a seafarer to get a simple, reliable answer: where they could go, how to reach the city, where to find help, and how to get back to the ship safely.

It became clear that this was not a local problem but an international one. Seafarers needed up-to-date information verified directly in port, and welfare organisations needed a more effective way to share practical experience, research and local data.

That is how the concept of IMWIRSA took shape: to bring research, international cooperation, local expertise and digital tools together into a single practical system.

One of the first outcomes of this approach was MWApp — a digital assistant for seafarers in port, built on Port Cards and on information verified with the help of the MWApp Port Coordinator network.

IMWIRSA continues to develop this model on a simple principle: solutions in seafarer welfare must start from the seafarer's real needs, and be tested in practice.

Why We Exist

Mission & Vision

Our mission is to help develop a modern, accessible seafarer welfare system built around real seafarer needs, by bringing together research, innovation, local expertise and international cooperation.

Our vision is an international welfare environment in which every seafarer, in any port of call, can access up-to-date, verified information, existing support services and practical solutions that match their real needs.

🛡️
Safety
Helping seafarers stay safer in port and ashore through access to up-to-date local information, clear routes and verified support contacts.
👥
People
Putting seafarers' real needs at the centre of research, service design and the evaluation of welfare solutions.
⚙️
Efficiency
Developing practical solutions that complement existing welfare infrastructure and help seafarers' centres, missions, unions and other organisations use their resources more effectively.
📊
Data
Basing service development and recommendations on research, seafarer feedback, local verification of information and analysis of change across the maritime industry.
🌐
Global Cooperation
Building international cooperation between welfare organisations, researchers, unions, port authorities and other partners, while preserving the value of each port's local experience.

Where We're Going

Roadmap 2026–2035

Stages are directional, not rigid — different regions and organisations move at different speeds. Anyone can join at any stage.

Stage 1 · 2026–2028 — Foundation & Model Validation

Building the first working international network

MWApp · Port Cards · the MWApp Port Coordinator network · local information verification · Research Observatory · research and seafarer feedback · partner network · piloting practical welfare solutions.

Goal: build and test a repeatable model that combines research, digital tools, local verification and practical support for seafarers.

Stage 2 · 2029–2032 — Scaling & Integration

Expanding the proven model and bringing partners together

Expanding the geographic reach of MWApp and Port Cards · growing the international MWApp Port Coordinator network · integrating local welfare services and partners · developing the Research Observatory further · rolling out practical solutions proven to work · sharing data and best practice between ports.

Goal: turn the model proven in Stage 1 into a sustainable international system connecting seafarers, port welfare services, researchers and partner organisations. Target: extend the network of ports covered by MWApp and Port Cards to 50+ by 2032.

Stage 3 · 2033–2035 — A Sustainable International Ecosystem

From a growing network to a sustainable system

A sustainable international network of MWApp and Port Cards · a fully developed MWApp Port Coordinator system · ongoing local verification · international cooperation and exchange of practice · research and monitoring of change · developing and adapting welfare solutions to seafarers' evolving needs.

Goal: build a sustainable international ecosystem able to continuously track changes in seafarers' needs, verify information on the ground, and turn research and practical experience into accessible seafarer welfare solutions.
