For Seafarers' Centres & Missions

Are you a seafarers' centre or mission?

Partner with IMWIRSA as part of an international network of organisations working in seafarer welfare. Your organisation keeps its full independence — name, management, funding and traditions remain yours. IMWIRSA helps expand your international visibility, exchange of experience, and opportunities for practical cooperation.

A Simple, Transparent Process

How Partnership Begins

Not required

Your Independence Stays Intact

  • No mandatory financial contribution
  • No restructuring your organisation
  • No change to your charter or governance
  • No exclusivity — keep working with other networks
  • No new software or hardware to buy
What's needed

Willingness to Collaborate

  • A contact person from your organisation
  • Willingness to provide and keep up to date information about your services
  • Availability to liaise with the MWApp Port Coordinator and IMWIRSA Central Office when needed
  • Voluntary participation — the format and scope of cooperation are agreed together
The Person Behind Every Port

MWApp Port Coordinator

A local representative who helps IMWIRSA check and keep up to date the information about a specific port. The coordinator works to IMWIRSA's shared methodology and liaises with IMWIRSA Central Office.

🔄

Keeps data current

Checks local information and passes updates through the IMWIRSA MWApp Bot — changes go live after verification by IMWIRSA Central Office.

🌐

Link to IMWIRSA

Liaises with IMWIRSA Central Office and, where useful, takes part in exchanging information and experience with other coordinators.

Recognition & Support Options

The form of engagement is shaped around the coordinator's role, the scope of local work, and the nature of the cooperation with IMWIRSA.

🏅

Recognition of Contribution

A coordinator's contribution may be recognised with an IMWIRSA certificate, a reference, or a feature of their work in the organisation's communications.

💶

Financial Support

For certain ports, IMWIRSA may engage coordinators on a paid basis — depending on the scope and nature of the local work required.

📈

Professional Involvement

The opportunity to take part in growing the coordinator network, exchanging practical experience, and improving the methodology for verifying port information.
